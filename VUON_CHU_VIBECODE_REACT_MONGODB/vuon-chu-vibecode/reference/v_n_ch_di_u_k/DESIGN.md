## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an enchanting, tactile, and nurturing outdoor classroom for children aged 4 to 8 discovering the Vietnamese language. The digital environment evokes the sensation of stepping into an illustrated forest picture book: sunny clearings, dew-kissed leaves, playful critter companions, and gentle breezes.

The visual style blends **Playful Tactility** with **Whimsical Organic Storytelling**:
- **Tactile Softness:** Buttons and interactive elements mimic springy moss cushions, smooth river stones, and juicy berries. Elements feature generous bevel-like bottom borders to simulate physical push-buttons that give young motor-skills instant feedback.
- **Friendly Companionship:** Forest animal guides (such as squirrels, owls, and sun bears) inhabit card frames and speech bubbles, providing supportive micro-copy and audible reassurance.
- **Joyful Clarity:** Interfaces avoid clinical minimalism and chaotic sensory overload. Instead, clean cream pastures, oversized interactive targets, and cheerful nature badges guide little learners without cognitive strain.

## Colors
The palette captures the vitality of an organic Vietnamese garden under bright morning sunlight, balancing playful vibrancy with high visual legibility.

- **Primary (Emerald & Leaf Greens):** `#059669` (Core), `#10B981` (Vibrant), `#34D399` (Light Mint), `#047857` (Pressed Shadow). Signifies discovery, growth, correct answers, and main action drivers.
- **Secondary (Sunshine Amber & Honey):** `#F59E0B` (Core Amber), `#FBBF24` (Warm Honey), `#FEF08A` (Pale Meadow). Evokes joy, rewards, star collections, and audio prompts.
- **Tertiary (Clear Sky Blue & Blossom Coral):** `#0284C7` (Sky Core), `#38BDF8` (Sky Tint), paired with `#FB923C` (Tangerine) and `#F43F5E` (Blossom Berry) for dynamic category coding (tones, vowels, consonants, activities).
- **Background Tones:** Soft cream and morning dew washes (`#F0FDF4`, `#FEFCE8`, `#F8FAFC`), avoiding sterile stark whites to reduce eye fatigue during learning sessions.
- **Text & Neutral Contrast:** Deep forest evergreen charcoal (`#064E3B` and `#0F172A`) ensures AAA contrast compliance across all interactive flashcards and instructions while retaining warm organic undertones.

## Typography
Typography is customized to support early-stage Vietnamese readers. Letterforms must render distinct Vietnamese diacritics (dấu sắc, huyền, hỏi, ngã, nặng, nón, râu) with uncrowded vertical headroom and rounded terminal curves.

- **Display & Headlines (Quicksand):** Round, plump geometric styling that provides an instantly welcoming, storybook feel. Generous line-height accommodates accent marks without collision.
- **Body & Instructions (Nunito Sans):** Selected for warm, open counters, tall x-height, and exceptional legibility at reading distances on tablets and phones.
- **Phonics & Tone Highlights:** When displaying standalone letters or tone markers, weight stays at bold (`700`) with colored accent highlights to help children map visual symbols to auditory phonemes.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a responsive, card-first fluid structure anchored around comfortable physical reach zones for young children:

- **Touch Margins & Hit Targets:** Interactive components require a minimum touch footprint of 56px × 56px (ideally 64px+ for primary sound and selection tiles) with comfortable dead-space to prevent accidental taps.
- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Mobile (< 640px):** Single-column stacked layouts, sticky bottom-anchored action bars with pill buttons, 16px side canvas padding.
  - **Tablet (640px - 1024px):** 2-to-3 column interactive playground grids, dual-pane flashcard arenas, 24px gutters.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** Maximum canvas width constrained to 1200px centered in a decorative pasture frame, retaining the intimacy of an oversized story mat.
- **Flow & Rhythm:** Vertical rhythm is deliberately generous (`space-md` to `space-xl`) to isolate visual choices, reducing decision paralysis for early learners.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in this system avoids harsh drop shadows and sterile grayscale blurs. It relies on **Tactile Meadow Tiers** and **Chunky 3D Pillows**:

- **Physical 3D Offset (Squishy Touch):** Primary interactive objects (buttons, tiles, flashcards) utilize solid downward borders (`4px` to `6px`) in a deeper shade of their surface color (e.g., `#047857` under `#10B981`). On tap or click, the card depresses via `transform: translateY(4px)`, compressing the border and giving tangible tactile satisfaction.
- **Ambient Sunny Glows:** Hover and floating elements cast soft, color-tinted shadows (e.g., `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.22)` or `0 12px 24px -6px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`).
- **Layered Papercut Canvases:** Surface containers sit upon background pasture colors using crisp, low-contrast border strokes (`2px solid #DCFCE7` or `#FEF08A`) rather than stark black dividers.

## Shapes
The design embraces organic, pill-shaped, and rounded contours (Level 3: Full Pill & Pebble curves). Sharp 90-degree corners do not exist in this design language:

- **Pebble Radii:** Standard interactive buttons, badges, and progress pills use complete stadium curvature (`rounded-full` / `9999px`).
- **Story Cards & Modals:** Flashcards and dialogue panels use ultra-soft corners (`rounded-2xl` / `1.5rem` to `rounded-3xl` / `2rem`), echoing natural forms like river pebbles, cloud puffs, and wooden blocks.
- **Decorative Organic Motifs:** Badges often incorporate gentle scalloped edges, leafy notches, and cloud lobes to maintain the whimsical outdoor motif.

## Components

### 1. Bubbly Buttons & Sound Triggers
- **Primary Action (Play / Next / Check):** Stadium pill button with vibrant leaf green fill (`#10B981`), bold white text, and a `4px` solid bottom ridge (`#047857`). On hover, gentle floating bounce; on active press, shifts down `3px`.
- **Sound Play Button:** Circular (68px × 68px) bright amber (`#FBBF24`) speaker button crowned with a white megaphone or musical note icon, accompanied by radial wave rings that ripple outwards during pronunciation playback.

### 2. Interactive Flashcards
- Pure white or soft cream card base, framed by a playful 3px border (`#DCFCE7`). 
- Features an illustrated nature object (e.g., "Quả Khế", "Con Sóc") in the upper tier, paired with phonetically distinct Vietnamese letters at `32px` bold.
- Includes a dedicated, high-contrast audio repeat button in the card corner and interactive flip/tap states with celebratory star particles upon successful identification.

### 3. Mascot Guidance Cards & Speech Bubbles
- Rounded rectangular dialogue containers (`24px` radius) with an organic pointer pointing toward the forest critter companion.
- Warm yellow-cream surface (`#FEFCE8`) bordered with honey gold (`#FDE047`), displaying bite-sized encouragement and clear spoken Vietnamese phrases.

### 4. Chips & Category Badges
- Pill-shaped badges (`rounded-full`) with pastel tint backgrounds (`#ECFDF5`, `#E0F2FE`, `#FFF7ED`) and saturated text labels (`#065F46`, `#0369A1`, `#C2410C`).
- Used to distinguish vowel groups, tone markers (Dấu), and vocabulary difficulty levels.

### 5. Input & Matching Selectors
- Large rounded tile selectors (minimum 64px height) with thick rounded borders.
- **Default:** Clean white background with `#E2E8F0` border.
- **Selected:** Light emerald tint (`#ECFDF5`) with 3px `#10B981` border and bouncy checkmark indicator.
- **Incorrect:** Gentle wobble animation with soft coral border (`#FB923C`), avoiding alarming reds.

### 6. Reward Indicators & Progress Trails
- **Star Counters:** Bubbly amber badges that collect glowing golden stars with gentle bounce animations upon task completion.
- **Forest Path Stepper:** Stepper navigation modeled as stepping stones winding through a green meadow, with completed stones turning into blooming flowers or ripe fruit.
